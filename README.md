# Restaurant OS

**A centralized operations platform for restaurant businesses.**

## Overview

Restaurant OS is a restaurant management platform in development. It is designed to give owners and operators one workspace for managing restaurant accounts and locations, with a foundation for future operational tools and connected business systems.

## Core Functionality

- **Account management:** View restaurant account details, plan information, and account status.
- **Multi-location support:** Organize restaurant locations under an account.
- **Dashboard:** See an overview of account details and location counts.
- **Administrative tools:** Support platform administration and account oversight.
- **Future modules:** Expand toward finance, inventory, operations, business insights, and integrations with point-of-sale and delivery systems.

## Technology Stack

- **Next.js and React** — application framework and interface
- **TypeScript** — application development
- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and backend services
- **Vercel** — intended deployment platform

## Current Status

Restaurant OS is under active development. The account dashboard and administrative foundation are present, while additional operational modules and integrations remain in progress.

The long-term goal is to help restaurant businesses organize locations, connect essential systems, and bring important operational information together.
